\section{11.1 Verified Contributions \& Explicit Technical Limitations}
Major Contributions: (1) Resolved sparse HER static-goal stagnation (Phase 11->12: 0\% -> 100\% DR0 at 600k-1M); (2) Diagnosed micro-scale OOD slip collapse and delivered zero-retraining adaptive shielding (+41.0 pp Held-Out DR4 to 98.0\% in Phase 14); (3) Built collision-free two-object PreGrasp sorting (50/50 scripted in Phase 15.2.5A) and unbiased progress-masked BC (75\% grasp / 45\% lift); (4) Delivered hierarchical options (30\% vs 0\%), oblique RGB-D unprojection (14.8 mm), predictive Kalman tracking (95-100\%), MuJoCo 4.0.1 parity verification, 32.41 us E-stop bounds, and a live Three.js/FastAPI Digital Twin workstation backed by 126 passing automated tests.
Principal Limitations: Full 2-object autonomous RL sorting (0\% in 16.6), learned compliant peg insertion (0\% full insert vs 53.33\% scripted), and cross-simulator MuJoCo transfer without retraining (5\% calibrated) remain open problems. No physical Panda hardware was executed.
