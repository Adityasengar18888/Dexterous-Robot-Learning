\chapter{Chapter 8: Cross-Phase Synthesis, Negative Results, and Provenance Analysis}
\section{8.1 Strict Provenance Comparison (`SCRIPTED` vs `BEHAVIOR\_CLONING` vs `LEARNED`)}
Conflating scripted kinematic heuristics with end-to-end neural reinforcement learning leads to misleading engineering claims. Across all 27 registered experiments, deterministic scripted controllers (`Phase 15.2.5A`: 100\% 2-obj sorting; `Phase 16.4`: 53.33\% compliant insertion) excel when exact geometric state and contact thresholds are known, yet brittle failure ensues outside calibrated bounds. Pure SAC+HER achieves 100\% DR0 at 600k–1M steps (`Phase 12`), suffers OOD slip collapse without runtime shielding (`Phase 13.5`: 57\% -> `Phase 14`: 98\%), and outperforms sparse HER under balanced Behavioral Cloning warm-starts at low sample budgets (`5B7`: 45\% lift vs `5B2`: 7\% lift).

\section{8.2 Value of Negative Results and Statistical Failure Taxonomy}
Four negative experimental outcomes fundamentally shaped system architecture: (1) Phase 11's 25x100k sparse 0\% plateau proved static-goal HER equilibria; (2) Phase 15.2.5B's 0\% hover at z=0.50 m identified strict XY tolerance cylinder deadzones; (3) Phase 15.2.5B.1's 78.4\% closed-gripper bias exposed how unmasked demo tails poison critic/actor rollouts; and (4) Phase 17.1's 98\% -> 5\% PyBullet-to-MuJoCo drop falsified any premature assumption that PyBullet DR4 success implies physical FCI hardware readiness.
